## 🌙 About me

I'm a B.Tech student at **IIIT Nagpur** (batch of 2028), based in **Bhopal, India**. I like building things that sit between research and real use: models that see, systems that navigate, and apps that people actually carry in their pockets.

- 🔭 Working on AI/ML, computer vision, and mobile products
- 🎪 **Ex-Lead of Estoria**, the cultural and events club at IIIT Nagpur. I learned to run a team and ship events at scale
- 🏆 Smart India Hackathon participant, with a competitive pitching background
- 🎯 Looking for **AI/ML and software engineering internships for Summer 2027**
- 📚 Currently going deeper into system design and transformer research
